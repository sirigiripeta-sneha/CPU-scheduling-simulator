While the Gantt chart plays: purple = process arrives, orange = process running, green = completion, turnaround and waiting time are fixed. Turnaround = Completion - Arrival. Waiting = Turnaround - Burst. CPU utilization = busy time / total schedule length (from time 0 to the last completion).
